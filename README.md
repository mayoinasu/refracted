REFRACTED

A horror puzzle game where you have to rotate the boxes to refract the light into another box until it reaches the sensor.

Demo video:

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a400ad85-2ec0-496a-a0d7-207af6a2e5bf

Link to play this game: https://mayoinasu.itch.io/refracted

**In this game, all you have to do is:**
* Rotate the boxes to refract the light into another box until it reaches the sensor.
* Solve the puzzle quickly before your character gets hungry. If they do, the game will restart from your last checkpoint.
* Eat food to restore your character's hunger bar.
* Read or interact with objects to uncover hints about the lore.

All of the assets are created by my friend: Prissyc and Nourah
